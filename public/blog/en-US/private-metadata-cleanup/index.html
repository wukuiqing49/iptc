Practical guide

How to inspect and clean photo metadata before sharing

Photos can contain time, device, location and identity information. Decide what should be shared, then inspect the cleaned copy.

Current version 1.4.0 · 2026-11-12

Inspect what the photo contains

Review EXIF, IPTC and XMP information for GPS, time, contact and creator details. Not every photo contains every field; a sample without GPS cannot demonstrate GPS removal.

Clean and check the copy

  1. Keep the original photo if you need it for your archive.
  2. Open the privacy cleanup workflow and select the photos.
  3. Read back the processed file after cleanup.
  4. Confirm that you are sharing the cleaned copy.

Understand RAW and format limits

Cleanup behavior varies by format. RAW files may retain camera make or model needed for decoding. An empty list of every possible field is not a universal success criterion, and cleanup does not guarantee anonymity.

Inspect the image too

Street signs, license plates, reflections and identifiable faces can reveal information even after location fields are removed. Check the image alongside its metadata.
