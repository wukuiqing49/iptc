Help and support

For support, include the steps you followed, photo format, Android version and app version. Do not send passwords, server credentials or unredacted private photos.

Current version 1.4.0 · Android 7.0 and later

Before you download

Where can I download the app?

Use the Google Play link on this page. The store title is Photo Metadata & IPTC Editor, published by AndroidManTou.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 and later. Devices, file formats and system permissions can affect specific workflows.

Why can’t I see a field after saving?

Read back the generated file in the app and make sure you are viewing the processed photo. Check whether your other viewer reads the same IPTC or XMP field.

What if an upload fails?

Check server connectivity, sign-in details and destination permissions, then inspect the failure shown in the queue. You need to provide and configure your own delivery server.
