Before you download

Is this a photo retouching app?

CaptionMeta focuses on photo metadata: captions, keywords, credits and copyright. Color correction and visual retouching are not its main purpose.

Do I have to upload photos to edit them?

Local metadata editing does not require a cloud upload. Server delivery is configured separately. Read the official privacy policy for account, purchase and other service data practices.

Can every field be edited in every format?

Reading, writing and cleanup support vary by file format. Test with a copy and read the generated file back; do not assume all formats support the same fields.

Does metadata cleanup make a photo anonymous?

No. The image itself can reveal identity or location. RAW files may retain camera information needed for decoding, so check both the image and its saved metadata.
