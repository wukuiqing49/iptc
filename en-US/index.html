Fits a mobile photo workflow

Keep repeated metadata entry, privacy checks, and file delivery in one Android tool.

  1. Capture a photo or select local images.
  2. Choose a preset and fill in captions, keywords, credits, and rights.
  3. Process the batch, with per-photo overrides where needed.
  4. Review the result and add processed files to the delivery queue.
