Privacy

Data practices

CaptionMeta processes photos and metadata through the Android workflow. Processed files may be sent to a user-configured server only when the user configures delivery and starts it.

The app can request camera, location, and media access for capture, GPS metadata, and photo selection workflows.

Integrated services

The project integrates Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, and Firebase for account, purchase, and app operation features. Review the full privacy policy and store data-safety declaration before publication.
